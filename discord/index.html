The ACE Model

Three Setups. Zero Guessing.

I FOMO. I revenge trade. I know that about myself. So I built a strategy where the rules make the decisions, not my emotions.

Setup 01

The London Sweep

Price sweeps the London session high or low on the 5-minute. Two candles close the other way. Anchor the volume profile and Fib, wait for the retrace to 0.5 / 0.588, take the continuation.

Setup 02

The Flip

Setup 1 isn't valid? Price re-sweeps the swing. Two candles close back through it, you anchor the other way and take the continuation in the new direction.

Setup 03

The Double Sweep

London high and low both get swept. All you wait for is a 50% retrace, then you trade in the direction price is already going.
